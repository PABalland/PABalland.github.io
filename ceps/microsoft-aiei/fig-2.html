Economic Specialization (Revealed Comparative Advantage)

Reading the figure. Colour within a row shows what a city does with AI, not how much it does. Reading horizontally across the three lenses of one industry shows whether a specialization appears in knowledge production and in capital allocation alike; a city green in patents but red in funding is producing knowledge it is not capitalising on.

RCA is a city's share of an industry's AI activity relative to its share of all AI activity in the sample. Values on very small denominators are unstable and are not interpreted individually. Hatched columns indicate data not yet loaded.
