# EU-level and provider briefing (English)

## Scope
- EU institutions and agencies as buyers (country code EU in PA1 rows).
- Ownership chains of providers active in several member states.
- US, UK and Chinese parents: filings, subsidiaries, stated commitments on data access.
- EU rules that shape dependency: Data Act switching and portability rules, EUCS certification (check adoption status), any cloud and AI development legislation (check whether proposed or adopted).

## Where relationships are disclosed
- TED award notices for EU institutions.
- EDPS decisions and opinions on institutions' use of cloud services.
- European Court of Auditors reports.
- SEC EDGAR (10-K Exhibit 21 subsidiaries, risk factors on government data requests), GLEIF parent records.
- Providers' transparency reports on government data requests.
