## Leads (training knowledge, NOT verified)
- The government's cloud policy allows public cloud under conditions; check the current version and any 2025 or 2026 update.
- The Tweede Kamer adopted motions in 2025 calling for less dependence on US cloud services; check follow-up.
- Solvinity, which hosts government identity services, was reported in 2025 to be acquired by a US group. Verify the deal and the government's response: a textbook control-country change.
- Leaseweb, KPN as domestic providers.
