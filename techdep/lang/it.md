## Where relationships are disclosed
- Consip (acquistinretepa.it), ANAC, TED.
- ACN qualification of cloud services.
- Corte dei conti reports, Garante decisions, interrogazioni parlamentari.
- Registro Imprese for ownership.

## Leads (training knowledge, NOT verified)
- PSN is run by a consortium reported to include TIM, Leonardo, CDP and Sogei, with technology partnerships with several hyperscalers. Verify current shareholders and partners.
- Aruba (domestic provider).
- Fastweb is owned by Swisscom: a clear case where provider HQ (IT) differs from control country (CH). Verify current structure.
- PNRR funds financed large migrations of local administrations to the cloud; award lists may be public.

## Structural vs naive queries
- Naive: "cloud sovrano Italia".
- Structural: "aggiudicazione migrazione cloud PSN comuni 2025", "servizi cloud qualificati ACN elenco fornitori 2026", "interrogazione dipendenza hyperscaler pubblica amministrazione risposta".
