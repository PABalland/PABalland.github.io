# Workflow: Scenario

Question shape: "If [provider or country j] cut off, restricted or were compelled to hand over [service/data], what breaks in [country i]?"

1. **Define the shock.** Service suspension, sanctions-driven cutoff of a specific buyer, stop in software updates, or a legal order for data access. Each hits different dimensions (E6, A8, F1).
2. **Map exposure.** Use the country-exposure workflow to list the public services and critical sectors that rely on providers controlled from j.
3. **Precedents.** Search for real cases where a provider suspended service to a government, organisation or individual for legal or political reasons. Cite them. Do not invent any.
4. **Contract terms.** Look for reported exit clauses, reversibility requirements (for example in the EU Data Act) and data portability obligations.
5. **Substitution time.** For each exposed service, is there a certified alternative already under contract, and how long did comparable migrations take?
6. **Timeline.** Build a day 1, week 1, month 1, year 1 view of impact, marking every step as sourced or reasoned.

Output: exposure table, timeline, and PA1 rows for the dimensions the scenario touches.
