# Workflow: Country Exposure

Question shape: "How dependent is [country or sector] on cloud providers controlled from [j]?"

1. **Scope it.** Public sector, a critical sector (health, finance, energy) or the whole economy. Say which, and do not mix them.
2. **National policy frame.** Find the current national cloud doctrine or strategy and what it requires for sensitive data. Record the date.
3. **Big contracts.** Search national portals and TED for the largest cloud and hosting awards since 2022. List buyer, provider, value if given.
4. **Framework agreements.** Identify central purchasing frameworks for cloud and which providers are on them. Remember: framework membership caps at MODERATE INFERENCE for any single buyer.
5. **Headline shares.** Look for any published market share for the country (analyst press releases, national regulator studies, competition authority market studies). Cite year and method.
6. **Domestic alternatives.** Which certified domestic or EU-controlled providers exist and have they won public contracts?
7. **Ownership check.** For the top providers found, confirm control country with a corporate record.

Output: a short exposure summary by control country (share of contracts found, not market share, unless a real share source exists), plus PA1 rows (A4 where a share source exists, A7 per contract, D2, F1).
